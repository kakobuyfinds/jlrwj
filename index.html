CSSBuy ordering checklist

  1. Find the seller’s link

    Open the HipobuyFind product page and locate its original seller listing. If you plan to use CSSBuy, check that its order form accepts the seller URL and loads the correct item and variant.

  2. Compare actual measurements

    For a hoodie, compare chest width and sleeve length with a garment you own. For footwear, use your measured foot length and the seller’s size chart. Confirm whether dimensions are in centimeters.

  3. Review warehouse QC

    If warehouse photos are available, check the size label, color, quantity and visible defects. Ask for a measurement photo if fit is uncertain, and check any return deadline before approving shipment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is an organized list of product finds that helps shoppers compare listings before ordering through an agent. This independent guide uses listings from HipobuyFind. Confirm seller-link compatibility in CSSBuy before paying.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check the item’s listing and your agent’s warehouse order page for available QC photos. For your own order, verify that the photos show the size and color you selected.

Do the catalog prices include shipping?

The USD figures are approximate item prices. Add domestic delivery, international shipping and any applicable fees or taxes using your own quotes.

Where does the Buy it now button take me?

It opens the HipobuyFind homepage. Choose a category there or use the catalog above to open an individual listing.
